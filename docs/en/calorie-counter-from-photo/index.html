FAQ

How accurate is counting from a photo?

A photo is an estimate: the AI identifies foods and an approximate portion. That's why the bot always shows the result before logging and lets you fix the weight in one tap. For precision, weigh it or pick it from the database.

Do I have to weigh my food?

No. A photo or description is enough to start; add grams when you want precision.

Does it count alcohol?

Yes, alcohol calories are counted.

Is it free?

Yes — no subscription, no ads, right in Telegram.
