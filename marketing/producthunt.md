## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt 👋
>
> I built trix because every fitness app I tried waited for me to open it — and I stopped
> opening them in week two. Telegram is already open all day, so trix lives there and messages
> you first on training days.
>
> What it does:
> • A 2-minute button interview → a plan around your days, equipment and injuries (home or gym)
> • One-tap set logging; it decides when to add weight and flags plateaus and deloads
> • A body map: each muscle's weekly load, recovery and 12-week trend — and plans that fix their
>   own imbalances (no back work? it adds a row before you see the plan)
> • Technique pictures for every exercise, and a form check from a video of your set
> • Macros from a photo of your plate
> • A trainer mode for coaches with clients
>
> It's free with no ads — it runs entirely on free tiers (Cloudflare Workers + D1) — and the code
> is MIT on GitHub if you want to run your own.
>
> I'd love to hear what's missing for your training. 🙏
